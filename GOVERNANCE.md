# Governance

## Constitutional Layer
New Reality is governed by:
1. `docs/NEW-REALITY-CONSTITUTION.md`
2. Project-specific charters
3. Operating rules
4. Daily procedures

**GLOBAL CONSTITUTION → PROJECT CHARTER → OPERATING RULES → DAILY ACTION**

## Human Agency
Humans retain ultimate authority over values, rights, consent, governance, high-impact decisions, irreversible decisions, and constitutional amendments.

AI is an intelligence and execution layer, not sovereign authority.

## Founder Phase
The founder initially acts as project owner, first moderator, first operator, product lead, systems architect, research coordinator, and decision owner. This is a bootstrap state, not the intended permanent structure.

## Separation of Duties
As projects grow, responsibilities should separate into Governance, Product, Engineering, Operations, Compliance, Finance, Moderation, Community, and Security.

Critical decisions should receive independent review when practical.

## Moderation
Moderation is an organizational control layer. Moderators may approve, reject, quarantine, escalate, annotate, request evidence, and pause unsafe processes. Consequential decisions should document their reasons. Audit history should not be silently erased.

## AI Governance
AI may research, summarize, classify, simulate, forecast, recommend, execute authorized tasks, monitor, detect anomalies, and preserve organizational memory.

AI must not invent evidence, invent legal authority, silently alter governance, bypass safety controls, override human authorization, or make irreversible high-impact decisions without required human control.

## Evidence and Decision Rights
Important decisions should preserve:

**Question → Evidence → Alternatives → Decision → Authority → Action → Result → Review**

Unknowns remain explicitly marked as unknown.

## Transparency
Publish appropriate information about mission, project state, milestones, finances, major decisions, evidence, risks, blockers, failures, and outcomes. Privacy, security, and legitimate confidentiality remain constraints.

## Community Participation
Projects should define appropriate ways community members can contribute through research, feedback, testing, funding, local knowledge, design, volunteering, or governance. Participation is not governance power unless that power is actually granted.

## Constitutional Change
**Proposal → Discussion → Evidence → Decision → Version → Implementation → Review**

Changes affecting human agency, safety, privacy, or governance receive heightened review.

## Project #001
Adaptive / NewRealityVending remains the first experimental project. Its domain-specific rules cover regulated products, physical retail, hardware, payments, customer verification, legal compliance, and venue operations while remaining subordinate to the constitutional layer.
