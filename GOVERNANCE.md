# Governance

## Founder phase

The founder is:

- project owner,
- first moderator,
- first operator,
- final owner of roadmap decisions.

## Separation of duties as the project grows

Eventually separate:

```text
Founder/Board
     │
     ├── Product
     ├── Engineering
     ├── Operations
     ├── Compliance
     └── Moderation
```

## Critical decisions

Regulated product deployment should require documented review by the appropriate licensed/legal/regulatory parties.

The AI system never becomes the legal authority.
