# Adaptive — Crowdfunding One-Page

## The idea

Adaptive is building an intelligent physical retail platform where a connected machine becomes a software-defined retail node.

The system combines:

**Smart hardware + AI + inventory intelligence + product transparency + compliance controls + human moderation.**

## The first goal

Build and validate **one** prototype.

Not 1,000 machines.

Not a giant warehouse.

Not a national network.

One machine that proves the model.

## What the first prototype must prove

- Customers can use it.
- Products can be presented clearly.
- Inventory can be monitored.
- Transactions can be measured.
- Machine health can be monitored remotely.
- Product documentation can be linked to each SKU.
- Compliance rules can stop a product from being offered when required.
- Unit economics can be calculated.

## Why cannabinoids?

Cannabinoid retail is a demanding test case because the regulatory environment is complex and changing.

If the infrastructure can operate responsibly in a regulated category, the technology can potentially expand to other jurisdiction-specific retail categories.

## The safety valve

If a particular cannabinoid/product path becomes unavailable, the platform should not collapse.

The catalog can change.

The machine remains.

The software remains.

The data architecture remains.

The compliance engine becomes more valuable.

## Founder model

The founder is initially the first moderator and first worker.

The project is intentionally designed to convert founder labor into:

- software,
- SOPs,
- automation,
- data,
- repeatable deployment.

## What supporters are funding

They are funding the engineering and validation of an infrastructure platform.

They are not being promised:

- guaranteed returns,
- regulatory approval,
- guaranteed commercialization,
- guaranteed availability of a particular cannabinoid.

## Transparency promise

The project will track:

- money raised,
- money spent,
- milestones,
- failures,
- blockers,
- pilot results.

The goal is to make the development process itself auditable.
