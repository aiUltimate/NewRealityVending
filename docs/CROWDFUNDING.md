# Crowdfunding Blueprint

## Objective

Raise enough capital to produce and validate a controlled MVP.

The campaign should not be positioned as a speculative bet on a specific cannabinoid.

It should be positioned as:

> **Building the infrastructure for transparent, intelligent, compliance-first physical retail.**

---

# Campaign Architecture

## 1. Hook

Physical retail is still mostly static.

Adaptive turns a physical retail machine into a connected software system.

---

## 2. Problem

Traditional small-format retail often lacks:

- real-time inventory intelligence,
- product transparency,
- adaptive catalogs,
- remote monitoring,
- structured feedback,
- automated compliance workflows.

---

## 3. Solution

Adaptive combines:

```text
Smart Hardware
+
Retail OS
+
AI
+
Product Digital Twins
+
Compliance Engine
+
Human Moderation
```

# Founder Story Structure

Use factual statements:

- Founder is building the first prototype.
- Founder initially performs moderation and operations.
- The project is intentionally starting small.
- The first goal is measurable validation.
- The company intends to publish progress and failures.

Avoid exaggerated claims such as:

- "This will revolutionize everything."
- "Guaranteed to succeed."
- "Guaranteed legal."
- "Guaranteed returns."

---

# Funding Tiers

## Minimum

Research + software simulation + hardware proof-of-concept.

## Target

Physical Pod + software + controlled pilot.

## Stretch

Second machine + better telemetry + stronger analytics.

## Larger stretch

Additional pilot locations and partner integrations.

Each tier must have a defined deliverable.

---

# Budget Transparency

Publish:

- amount raised,
- platform fees,
- payment processing,
- taxes where applicable,
- hardware,
- software,
- legal,
- insurance,
- testing,
- inventory,
- operating reserve.

Maintain a public `SPENDING-LEDGER.csv` once funds are raised.

---

# Crowdfunding Risk Disclosure

The campaign must clearly state that:

- regulations can change,
- a specific product may become unavailable,
- a machine may require redesign,
- a partner may not approve deployment,
- fundraising does not guarantee commercialization,
- supporters are not promised investment returns unless a properly structured securities offering is used,
- regulated product rewards are subject to applicable law and platform rules.

Have campaign language reviewed before publication.
