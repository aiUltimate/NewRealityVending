# New Reality Constitution — v0.1

## Core Idea
New Reality is a human-AI community infrastructure framework for creating organizations that can learn, govern themselves, remain transparent, protect human agency, and continuously evolve.

The objective is not to build one perfect company. It is to build a system capable of repeatedly creating better projects, products, organizations, and institutions.

**NewRealityVending / Adaptive is Project #001: the first concrete laboratory.**

## Core Ethos
- **Moderation** — balance competing forces rather than allowing one optimization target to dominate.
- **Love** — treat human dignity, wellbeing, autonomy, relationships, and long-term consequences as real operating variables.
- **Evolution** — continuously learn, experiment, measure, and improve.
- **Transparency** — make important decisions, resources, evidence, risks, and failures understandable and auditable.
- **Human Agency** — AI expands human capability but does not become sovereign over people.

## Shared Organizational DNA
**Purpose → People → Values → Governance → Moderation → Intelligence → Transparency → Economics → Operations → Feedback → Evolution → Memory → Safety → Interoperability**

Every project inherits this DNA, then specializes it for its domain and jurisdiction.

## Human Agency
People remain the ultimate source of values, rights, consent, and legitimate governance. AI may research, summarize, model, simulate, detect, forecast, recommend, execute authorized tasks, monitor systems, and preserve organizational memory.

AI must not silently become the final authority over human rights, organizational values, consent, legitimate governance, or irreversible high-impact decisions.

## Moderation
Moderation is the organizational immune system. It includes ethical, financial, AI, community, operational, risk, power, and growth moderation.

It balances:
- Profit ↔ Community
- Freedom ↔ Safety
- Innovation ↔ Stability
- Automation ↔ Human Judgment
- Speed ↔ Evidence
- Growth ↔ Sustainability

## Transparency
Transparency is infrastructure. Important systems should explain what happened, why, who or what decided, what evidence was used, what resources were used, what risks exist, what failed, what changed, and what remains unknown.

The long-term goal is a **Public Reality Layer** containing appropriate portions of mission, state, finances, decisions, evidence, experiments, risks, failures, governance, AI activity, milestones, and future plans.

Transparency must coexist with privacy and security.

## Evidence
Keep distinct: verified fact, source material, expert interpretation, AI inference, community report, hypothesis, prediction, and decision. Important decisions should preserve their evidence trail.

## Evolution
**Observe → Identify → Hypothesize → Simulate → Experiment → Measure → Compare → Decide → Update → Document → Teach the next project**

A failed experiment becomes organizational value when the organization captures what happened and improves future decisions.

## Organizational Memory
The long-term **New Reality Knowledge Graph** should preserve knowledge, decisions, experiments, failures, successful patterns, research, legal findings, technical solutions, supplier information, economic models, community feedback, and operating procedures.

## Community
The community is a participant, not merely a funding source.

**Community → Ideas / Capital / Skills / Research / Testing / Local Knowledge → Project → Results → Community → Feedback → Evolution**

## Economics
Projects require revenue, capital, labor, ownership, reserves, compensation, maintenance, risk management, and growth capital.

The governing question is:

> How do we create durable economic value while remaining consistent with purpose, dignity, safety, evidence, and community trust?

## Governance
**GLOBAL CONSTITUTION → PROJECT CHARTER → OPERATING RULES → DAILY ACTION**

Projects define authority, responsibilities, decision rights, escalation, conflict resolution, audit mechanisms, succession, and amendment procedures.

## AI Governance
**Human Intent → AI Research → AI Analysis → AI Simulation → Human Decision → AI Execution → Real World → Data → AI Reflection → Human Governance → Evolution**

Automation may expand when evidence shows a task is sufficiently safe, deterministic, reversible, and auditable. High-impact or irreversible actions require stronger human controls.

## Safety
Critical uncertainty follows:

**UNKNOWN → PAUSE → RESEARCH → REVIEW → DECIDE**

Safety includes physical safety, legal compliance, cybersecurity, privacy, financial controls, AI reliability, operational resilience, abuse prevention, and trust.

## Privacy
Transparency does not mean surveillance. Collect the minimum useful information, protect sensitive information, define retention, document data use, avoid unnecessary profiling, and provide meaningful control where feasible.

## Interoperability
Prefer modular systems and avoid unnecessary lock-in between hardware, software, suppliers, data, AI providers, jurisdictions, payment systems, and community platforms.

## Project Lifecycle
**Question → Purpose → Research → Hypothesis → Design → Simulation → Experiment → Evidence → Decision → Deployment → Measurement → Learning → Iteration**

Projects should scale only when evidence justifies scaling.

## Project #001
Adaptive / NewRealityVending is the first experimental implementation. Its Legal Engine, Product Digital Twin, Adaptive OS, Adaptive Pod, AI, Analytics, Audit System, Moderator System, Crowdfunding, and Venue System are now understood as one domain-specific implementation of the broader New Reality architecture.

## Foundational Decision Questions
1. What are we trying to accomplish?
2. Who is affected?
3. What do we actually know?
4. What do we not know?
5. What evidence supports the decision?
6. What alternatives exist?
7. What are the risks?
8. Is the action reversible?
9. Who has legitimate authority to decide?
10. How will the result be measured?
11. What will we learn?
12. How will the learning improve the wider system?

## Amendment
**Proposal → Discussion → Evidence → Decision → Version → Implementation → Review**

No principle becomes sacred merely because it was written first.

## North Star
> **Build systems that help humans and AI create a more capable future without sacrificing human agency, dignity, transparency, safety, or the ability to evolve.**
