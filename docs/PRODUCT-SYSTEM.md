# Product System

## Product Philosophy

The product is not just an ingredient.

It is:

```text
Formulation
+
Documentation
+
Batch
+
COA
+
Legal status
+
Customer objective
+
Price
+
Machine compatibility
+
Evidence
```

---

# Objective Taxonomy

Initial non-medical objectives:

- CALM
- EVENING
- RECOVERY
- FOCUS
- FLAVOR
- HYDRATION
- GENERAL_WELLNESS

Objectives are navigation categories, not diagnoses.

# Product Digital Twin

Every SKU must have:

```text
Identity
Composition
Batch
Evidence
Legal status
Economics
Inventory
Customer feedback
```

---

# Formulation Intelligence

Potential future system:

```text
Product attributes
       ↓
Customer feedback
       ↓
Observed purchase behavior
       ↓
Objective mapping
       ↓
Demand model
       ↓
Candidate formulation
```

Important separation:

```text
Observed customer preference
≠
Clinical efficacy
```

The system should never turn consumer preference data into a medical efficacy claim.

---

# Private Label Path

### Stage 1
Existing compliant products.

### Stage 2
Contract manufacturing.

### Stage 3
Proprietary formulation.

### Stage 4
Own manufacturing only when economics and regulation justify it.

---

# Supplier Requirements

Supplier records:

- legal business name,
- address,
- contact,
- product catalog,
- batch records,
- COAs,
- testing laboratory,
- insurance,
- manufacturing credentials,
- certifications,
- ingredient documentation,
- recall procedure.

---

# Batch Traceability

```text
Supplier
 ↓
Raw material
 ↓
Batch
 ↓
Finished SKU
 ↓
Machine
 ↓
Transaction
```

A recall should be traceable in reverse:

```text
Batch
 ↓
SKUs
 ↓
Machines
 ↓
Transactions
 ↓
Affected events
```
