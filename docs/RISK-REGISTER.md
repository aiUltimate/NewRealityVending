# Risk Register

## Critical risks

### Regulatory
- cannabinoid classification,
- federal hemp changes,
- PA dispensing requirements,
- product approval,
- venue authorization.

### Business
- weak demand,
- low margin,
- high venue share,
- hardware capex,
- supplier dependence.

### Technology
- payment failures,
- machine failures,
- connectivity,
- cybersecurity,
- AI hallucination.

### Operations
- restocking,
- theft,
- tampering,
- customer disputes,
- recalls.

### Reputation
- unsupported claims,
- opaque sourcing,
- privacy incident,
- compliance violation.

---

# Risk Score

```text
Risk Score = Probability × Impact
```

Use a 1–5 scale for each.

```text
1–4    Low
5–9    Moderate
10–16  High
17–25  Critical
```

This is an internal management scale, not a scientific probability estimate.

---

# Risk Response

```text
AVOID
REDUCE
TRANSFER
ACCEPT
```

Critical legal/safety risks should generally be avoided or reduced rather than accepted.
