# Contributing

Adaptive is initially founder-led.

## Contribution model

All contributions should improve one or more of:

- legal robustness,
- technical reliability,
- customer value,
- transparency,
- unit economics,
- scalability.

## Research contributions

Every regulatory claim should include:

- source,
- date checked,
- jurisdiction,
- relevant section,
- uncertainty.

## Code contributions

Requirements:

- tests,
- documentation,
- clear commit message,
- no secrets,
- no hard-coded credentials,
- no bypasses around compliance gates.

## Product contributions

Do not add medical claims without appropriate evidence and review.
